# Document Capture Guide

Final assets: `public/ocr/capture-guide-badge.webp` and
`public/ocr/capture-guide-id.webp` (330 x 440 pixels each).
Created using the built-in image generation tool, then exported as static WebP.
The user's photographs were used only as posture and orientation references.
No real portrait or personal document data is reproduced or published.
The hand-detection feature was canceled. These images are instructional only.

## Generation Prompt

### Employee Badge

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a new clean instructional illustration
for a mobile form. Use the reference photograph only for the hand pose and
vertical card orientation. Do not reproduce real portraits, names, numbers,
logos or personal data. One natural medium-brown adult hand is gently cupped,
palm facing the viewer, holding a portrait-orientation generic employee badge
flat against the palm. Fingers are naturally together and curled behind the
card, with fingertips visible to the right/top and the thumb to the left.
No fingertip covers the badge front. All four corners are visible. The white
badge has a dark-green generic silhouette, gray placeholder lines and an empty
number rectangle. Simplified realistic educational illustration with soft
shading and correct anatomy, wrist entering from bottom left. Light cool gray
background, no keyboard, desk, second card, screen, phone, strap or other objects.

### Identity Card

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a new clean instructional illustration
for a mobile identity-document form. Use the reference photograph only for hand
pose and landscape card orientation. Do not reproduce real portraits, names,
numbers, dates, signatures, government symbols or personal data. One natural
medium-brown adult hand is gently cupped, palm facing the viewer, holding a
landscape-format generic identity card flat against the palm. The thumb is to
the left and fingers are together behind the card, visible beyond the right
edge and below it. No finger covers any printing. All four corners are visible.
The off-white card has a dark-green generic silhouette and gray placeholder
lines, without readable text or numbers. Simplified realistic educational
illustration, soft shading, correct anatomy, wrist entering from bottom left,
light cool gray background, no keyboard, desk, computer, phone or second document.
